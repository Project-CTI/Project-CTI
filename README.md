# Project CTI

### Making critical thinking something we cherish and pursue, not something **we** avoid. 

**Project CTI (Critical Thinking Initiative)** is an open project exploring
mathematics, science, visualization, and community-driven learning in order
to mitigate current decline in both communal spirit and drive to learn. 

How? Build things. Things that 
    - People love to use!
    - People find pretty!
    - People can't resist

The pursuit of wisdom is a gift we should all pursue and encourage others too
as well. 

CTI is a collection of experiments, visualizations, tools, and community
projects designed to make reasoning more accessible, and more fun.

------- 

## 📚 What is CTI? 🧠

AI can give us answers incredibly quickly. Quicker than we can understand. 

That makes it even more important that we understand how to:

- ask better questions
- recognize patterns
- interpret evidence
- communicate ideas
- reason through uncertainty
- think for ourselves
- and MOST **IMPORTANTLY** stay curious, no matter what. 

CTI is my attempt to build a place where those things are practiced and cherished

## Dash Math

**A new frontier of universal understanding**

Dash Math turns mathematical ideas and real-world datasets into interactive
visualizations, animations, experiments and questions. 

Instead of just looking at a number, we can:

**see it → manipulate it → question it → understand it → collaborate on it**

Topics can range from simple patterns to genuinely strange ones.

> Mathematics doesn't have to stay on the page.

# 👋 Why I'm Building This

A world without curiousity is not one I want to take part in.

I enjoy learning about how computers learn, outperform and eventaully
will surmise humans in pretty much any mental capacity. But, more than that,
I enjoy remembering the purpose of that learning. To connect, to teach,
to learn, to collaborate and most of all to enjoy mental stimulation
as a community.
